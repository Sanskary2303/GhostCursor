# Shopping Cart Information

The shopping cart displays all items currently selected for purchase along with quantities, sizes, and pricing.
Standard shipping is $8.99, but free shipping applies automatically to all orders over $75.
Applicable sales tax is calculated at 8% on the order subtotal.
Customers can enter the promotional code SAVE20 to receive a 20% discount on their cart subtotal.
Only one promo code may be applied per order.
Items can be removed from the cart at any time before proceeding to checkout.
Proceeding to checkout requires providing a valid shipping address and contact details.
The order summary updates dynamically to reflect applied discounts, taxes, and shipping fees.
