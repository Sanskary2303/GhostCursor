# Order Tracking and Returns

Customers can track the progress of their purchases using their order ID.
Orders progress through several stages: Processing, Shipped, Out for Delivery, and Delivered.
Once an order has shipped, a carrier tracking number is assigned to monitor shipment updates in real time.
ShopHive offers a 30-day return policy from the original purchase date for unworn items in original packaging.
To initiate a return or exchange, contact customer support with your order number.
Refunds are processed back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days following inspection.
If you have questions regarding order status or delivery delays, our support team is available Monday through Friday.
